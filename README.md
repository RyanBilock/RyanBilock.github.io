# Ryan Bilock — Aerospace Engineering Portfolio

**Live site: [ryanbilock.github.io](https://ryanbilock.github.io)**

Personal portfolio of Ryan Bilock, aerospace engineering student at the University of South Carolina and incoming NASA Pathways intern at Marshall Space Flight Center. It covers research in flight dynamics, mission design, and space systems, along with military service and technical skills.

## What's on the site

- **About:** background, research, and career focus
- **Experience:** NASA Pathways (Marshall Space Flight Center), the Adaptive Real-Time Systems Laboratory, NASA L'SPACE Mission Concept Academy, and the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA)
- **Projects:** SWIFT-UAV six-degree-of-freedom flight simulation, the Khonshu I lunar south pole mission concept, and the AstroTarget astrophotography dashboard
- **Military service:** South Carolina Army National Guard and Combined Joint Task Force–Horn of Africa
- **Skills and contact:** tools, methods, email, and LinkedIn
- **Resume:** downloadable PDF

## Repository contents

## Updating

- **New resume:** upload the new PDF named exactly `resume.pdf` so it replaces the old one.
- **Content changes:** edit `index.html` and commit. GitHub Pages redeploys automatically within a few minutes.
